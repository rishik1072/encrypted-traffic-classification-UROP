## 1. Audit Framework & Evidence Taxonomy

To establish an authoritative scientific baseline before beginning research upgrades, every major claim made across project documentation, research notes, and the Phase 0 report is classified into one of five mutually exclusive evidentiary categories:

- **`VERIFIED`**: Directly supported by unambiguous source code implementation and preserved, reproducible experimental artifacts.
- **`PARTIALLY VERIFIED`**: Empirical evidence or implementation exists, but the scope, assumptions, or operational constraints are incompletely documented or nuanced.
- **`UNVERIFIED`**: Reported in tables, manifests, or narrative texts, but lacking underlying raw experimental artifacts, execution logs, or source code backing.
- **`CONTRADICTED`**: The physical source code, mathematical implementation, or raw experimental data directly conflicts with the statement.
- **`PROPOSED`**: Formally identified as a future research objective, aspirational design, or roadmap item; not currently implemented or measured.

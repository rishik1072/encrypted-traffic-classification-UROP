## 1. Claims Governance Policy

This ledger provides an exhaustive forensic accounting of every public claim, headline metric, and scientific conclusion stated across `README.md`, `results/`, and `docs/paper/`.

### Verification Criteria
- **VERIFIED (REAL):** Proven empirically on real captured traffic under leakage-free group-aware evaluation with adequate statistical power.
- **VERIFIED (SYNTHETIC ONLY):** Empirically verified to reproduce, but strictly on synthetic fixture data (`dataset_v1`). Must never be claimed as representative of real network traffic.
- **UNVERIFIED / CONTRADICTED:** The claimed metric is contradicted by empirical logs, mathematically distorted, or unachievable under real-world conditions.
